# Tafseer-Additional-3

Tafseers and translations of the meanings in languages that have no tafseer
in QUL — used by the Tahfeez Al Quran app. One folder per item, 30 juz files
each (`1.json` … `30.json`, `"sura:aya"` → text as `<p>` paragraphs; footnotes
are their own paragraphs). Each repo stays under 40 MB (jsDelivr serves at most
50 MB per repo); items continue in the next Tafseer-Additional-N repo.

| Folder | Language | Title in the app | Source | Version |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `dhivehi-presidents-office/` | Dhivehi | Translation of the meanings – Office of the President of Maldives | [QUL](https://qul.tarteel.ai/resources/translation/86) / Quran.com translation 86 | QUL copy |
| `dhivehi-abu-bakr-ibrahim/` | Dhivehi | Translation of the meanings – Abu Bakr Ibrahim Ali | [QUL](https://qul.tarteel.ai/resources/translation/840) / Quran.com translation 840 | QUL copy |
| `marathi-ansari/` | Marathi | अर्थांचा अनुवाद – मुहम्मद शफी अन्सारी | [QUL](https://qul.tarteel.ai/resources/translation/226) / Quran.com translation 226 | QUL copy |
| `sindhi-amroti/` | Sindhi | معنيٰ جو ترجمو – تاج محمود امروٽي | [QUL](https://qul.tarteel.ai/resources/translation/238) / Quran.com translation 238 | QUL copy |

## Credit and terms

Translations from the Encyclopedia of the Translations of the Meanings of the
Holy Quran (QuranEnc.com), mostly by the Rowwad Translation Center.
Re-published under QuranEnc's terms: the text is not modified, the source is
cited, and each item is kept to its latest version.

Built by `scripts/tafseer/import_quranenc.py` in the app repo.
